What this model does

  1. Insurance: Payouts to designated beneficiaries or estate
  2. WROS: Joint ownership with survivorship transfers
  3. Designations: Recorded beneficiary designations for assets
  4. Trusts: Recorded trust transfers
  5. Probate: Probate inventory estimate of remaining assets
  6. Obligations: Modeled payment of entered costs and debts
  7. Residuary: Allocation of the net estate to residuary beneficiaries
  8. Warnings: Liquidity and solvency warnings
